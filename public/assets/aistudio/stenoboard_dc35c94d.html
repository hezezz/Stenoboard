How the keys work & quiz guide
  • A steno stroke is every key you hold at once. It is sent when you release them all, so press the whole chord together.
  • Top row on laptop: Q W E R = S T P H, T Y = star (*), U I O P [ = F P L T D.
  • Home row: A S D F = S K W R, G H = star (*), J K L ; ’ = R B G S Z.
  • Thumbs: C V = A O, N M = E U. Any number key is the number bar (#).
  • Star (*) on its own: Deletes the last word typed, like the classic correction mark.
  • Quizzes available: Sentence practice (type real phrases word-by-word), Steno Abbreviation briefs quiz, Days of the Week, and Months of the Year.
  • Hardware note: Many laptop keyboards can only read 4 to 6 keys simultaneously. If chords drop keys, an external NKRO keyboard or screen multi-touch is recommended.
